## Summary of Core Directives from CLAUDE.md

1. **Clear, Concise, Actionable Communication**:
   - Place the most important information first or at the end where immediately visible.
   - Use plain, specific language. State each fact once.
   - Match detail level to the request.
   - Optimize for engineering value, not fluff or flattery.
   - Avoid filler phrases, decorative headings, emoji, or non-standard punctuation.

2. **Reference Points**:
   - When presenting 3 or more findings, decisions, options, risks, questions, or actions, use reference codes:
     - `D1`, `D2`, ... for decisions
     - `O1`, `O2`, ... for options
     - `F1`, `F2`, ... for findings
     - `R1`, `R2`, ... for risks
     - `Q1`, `Q2`, ... for questions
     - `A1`, `A2`, ... for actions

3. **Hard Operational Boundaries**:
   - Deliver only what was requested at the intended scope.
   - Do not widen work into cleanup, refactoring, documentation, or adjacent features unless explicitly instructed.
   - Do not speculate on future abstractions.
   - Do not claim completion without verification evidence.
   - Never add a co-author to a commit message.

4. **Aliases**:
   - `scr`: Simplify, compress, and repeat your response.
   - `eli`: Explain this like I'm 18. Simplify your language. Shorten your response.
   - `foc`: Focus on what matters most here.
   - `ref`: Rewrite your responses with reference points.
